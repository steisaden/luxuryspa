# Landing experience surface brief

- Scope: `/` commercial landing experience; visitor mode: Persuade with an Experience-led first viewport.
- Audience and job: resort decision-makers evaluating whether “The Exhale” deserves a private concept walkthrough.
- Action and proof: understand the one-take towel → oil → steam → mineral-water treatment world, then submit the server-validated inquiry form. The film is the only supplied proof asset; do not invent clients, metrics, awards, pricing, or availability.
- Direction: “Descent and return.” A sticky full-bleed film plane begins in warm macro texture, descends through four document chapters into deep mineral color, and returns to a quiet candlelit commercial close. The memorable moment is the film slipping beneath the waterline while document color and copy move from warm stone into deep water.
- Constraints: Fastify/Nunjucks SSR, HTMX only for document/fragment/form exchange, native TypeScript/GSAP/ScrollTrigger/Lenis for local motion, complete no-JavaScript fallback, reduced motion, keyboard and screen-reader support, mobile reflow, no client router or virtual DOM.
- Open decisions: final brand owner, external inquiry destination, property-specific offer, pricing, availability, and production analytics.

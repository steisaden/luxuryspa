## Design Specificity Verdict

The experience is recognizably The Exhale rather than a category-interchangeable spa template. The towel → oil → steam → mineral-water sequence governs the film, chapter language, panel tint, progress line, return section, and motion cadence. Specificity was strongest in material storytelling and weakest at conversion, where the initial lead-form language did not explain the resort-team value as clearly as the film explained the treatment atmosphere.

The deterministic source scan returned `[]` with exit code 0: zero findings, rules, or file locations. Browser injection succeeded and initially reported five runtime categories: `all-caps-body`, `cramped-padding`, `repeated-section-kickers`, and `cream-palette` (the detector groups multiple affected nodes). The all-caps and repeated-kicker signals were valid and were removed. The button had a 48px minimum target but zero computed block padding; explicit block padding was added. `cream-palette` is an intentional false positive because steam ivory and warmed towel surfaces are extracted from the supplied film and documented in DESIGN.md.

## Overall Impression

A convincing cinematic descent with real material specificity. The largest opportunity was to make the business offer and guided-viewing sequence as intentional as the film chapters.

## What’s Working

1. The sticky native film and chapter copy create one continuous towel/oil/vapor/water passage rather than a generic section stack.

3. Progressive enhancement is visible in the product experience: links, chapter content, inquiry validation, and archive remain meaningful without JavaScript.
